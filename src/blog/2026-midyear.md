## killing spotify
![](/_includes/img/spotify.jpg)

if i'm being honest, my aversion to spotify didn't come from a place of "evil company bad" as much as the frankly *horrendous* user experience of using it and constant enshittification of the interface over a decade. i always wanted to go back to using foobar and alternatives, but with how little storage my phone has and how much music i listen to- there's not really a reality where i don't need to manage multiple devices and libraries as the alternative. my desire to move also came from a realisation of just how little intentional album listening i've been doing, and instead usually just a few standout tracks get added to a playlist or my liked songs and the rest get thrown to the sands of time, which is a shame because albums are such a fantastic experience and especially when presented as a cohesive unit rather than a collection of singles. 

### killing spotify *with cds!*
![](/_includes/img/cdplayer.jpg)

since picking up this shitty cd player that every school in my area had growing up, i've made it my mission to add picking up cheap cds to my opshopping rotation, and boy am i glad for it. having a seperate setup in the living space that i can just hit play on without touching my pc has been awesome, and it's a great conversation piece and piece of tactile tech that incidentally also forces me to find whacky and forgotten new music and listen to it very intentionally. just having a cd on while i cook makes me feel very warm and fuzzy, it's simple and nice.

### killing spotify *with the hiby r1!*
![](/_includes/img/hiby.jpg)

finally bit the bullet and picked up the hiby r1 and zero-ones as my new everyday music carries! if not for soulseek and musicbee, honestly i'm not sure i would've ever fully committed this hard to transferring to a personal library over streaming, but thank fucking goddd for them because moving to a tertiary device for music has been so good. a lot of times when i was listening to music on my phone, considering i already had earphones in i could just swap to some short form content when i was bored- but since i use a pair of 3.5mm earphones on my hiby r1, and my phone doesn't have a 3.5mm jack- i end up never really doing that and instead just listening to music and being a bit more bored (/pos) and listening to MANY more albums than i usually ever do.

the zero-ones get a glowing endorsement too, i'm not much of an audiophile but these sound great, have a really clever earhook that make them stay in even on long bike rides, feel STURDY, and look pretty fantastic! also they are CHEAP for something of this quality.

### top 5 cds
![](/_includes/img/cds.jpg)

in no particular order:
1. **gatecrasher - discotech**

i would be lying if i said that i *didn't* just get this cd at the bargain bin because it looked fucking sick and has an incredible design on the case/cds themselves. however, it is also a really solid techno/trancey cd that is *pretty fucking long* and so much so that i haven't been able to finish it, but everything i've heard has been solid and a good in for me to get more into oldschool techno since there are defs some elements i'd really love to see done a bit more in other music!

2. **frou frou - details**

if you don't know imogen heap/frou frou, i am really sorry for your loss. finding this cd for like $5 was such a pull and it's just such a phenomenal pop album that punches hard and has some really satisfying and fascinating harmonies without edging into niche or harder listening. easiest recommendation for anyone honestly!

3. **james yorkston - year of the leopard**

this was a complete random find in the $2 bargain bin and caught my attention from the gorgeous cover art as well as potentially being a folky album for me to put on while cooking. i was really worried this album was gonna be just a singer songwriter with a soft-spoken male voice and a guitar, but was so pleasantly surprised for the amount of depth on a lot of these tracks musically. i'm HORRIBLE at paying attention to lyrics, but it has this sense of melancholic wistfulness that catches me just sitting on my couch and staring out at the sunset. it never goes into anything far-fetched, but it definitely plays within the genre with interesting instrumentation and some gentle vocal melodies and a lovely sweet album i've been cuddling to a bit.

4. **wheat - hope and adams**

never heard of this artist or album either, but similar to year of the leopard it was picked up for looking like a nice easy indie album. another sleeper hit! a lot of these tracks wouldn't be amiss in a coming of age teen film (in a good way) like *don't hold you* but then we have some banjo-y country style instrumentation on *body talk*?? once again, nothing groundbreaking or experimental but just a great indie-sounding album akin to a weezer.

5. **cocteau twins - heaven or las vegas**

i never actually also never heard of the cocteau twins until last year when my ex introduced me to them through the playlist they made for me. i saw the cd (again, in the bargain bin) and felt a horrible pang in my chest when i listened to a track on youtube before picking the cd up and realised the track i opened was the opening track to that playlist. this story really doesn't have much to do with the album, the album is great and i have been putting it on whenever i have guests for a chill vibe and i'm sure nobody has anything new to add about their take on cocteau twins, but like another cursed object i don't listen to the album by myself unless i'm really trying to put myself into a pit. it's just fascinating how strong association can weigh on the most mundane things.

### top 5 albums on my hiby r1
![](/_includes/img/ipod.gif)

most of the stuff i've been listening to has been on bike rides, so they definitely have a certain vibe but still here are SCIENTIFICALLY the most played 5 albums on my mp3 player! note that i don't have any albums i own on cd on my digital library, there's no real rationale aside from i like being weird.

1. **the softies - holiday in rhode island**

*your hand is mine so suddenly*
*when did you find this good in me* 
*it's just the day that you don't want to end*
*tomorrow you'll fall out of love again.*

i don't actually remember where i found this album, but i'm glad i did. this year has been just very emotionally tumultuous and this album is full of so much gentle yearning that feels like it feels like a warm hug from someone you won't see for a long time. the vocals are so soft and gentle but have such a hold on me along some of the roughness of the album and the ethereal quality of how echoey and space-y it sounds, it just creates an album i constantly come back to to fill my chest with air.

2. **mrvoletron - assorted wow remixes**

yes. wow as in world of warcraft. hear me the FUCK out okay!! mrvoletron remixes have kinda been a staple of my wow friend group for over a decade, and now that i'm not bound to official streaming services, i downloaded these remixes in case they get taken down again and oh boy. i still like them.

you can totally write off a lot of these songs as gag songs, stuff like [you face jaraxxus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S5tUfbMIkzU) that got stupid popular even outside of wow circles but one of my favourites has always been [algalon the observer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBtMb_4KnAo) which is just a nice lounge track with a groovy melody. however a new favourite is the [onyxia wipe remix](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JpZDLR1be8I). just writing this is making me blush with embarassment, but there is something about the 'game fx and joke album' jesters we had in the 2010s that is so charming and brought me back to listening to some ocremix tracks as well as revisting stuff like yung scrolls's tes mixes. i hope mrvoletron is still making music, wherever he is.


3. **similar kind - good grief!**

okay back to serious albums, and this is cheating a bit since i have listened to this album a lot before getting my r1. i originally found the band from a hxh tiktok edit (lmao) but this album has comfortably landed as one of my favourites with a cool retro rock vibe with some party brass and a really interesting vocal synergy with the instrumental sound. it sounds between eras in a fun and interesting way, plus a lot of the songs have some CATCHYY tunes and lyrics even among the melancholic tracks. i've had a lot of compliments on putting this album on, and frankly i look forward to seeing what else they do!

4. **kaleidoscopio - tem que valer**

THIS ALBUM IS SO FUN!!! you will NOT have a bad day if you ride your bike to it at all, it feels like a latin mario kart soundtrack and will never leave my portable library EVER! the album is energetic, has an insane groove, and is SO bouncy without entering 'driving my car off a bridge' territory. you gotta listen to this on a sunny day and see what i mean.

5. **school food punishment - riff-rain**

school food punishment is so incredibly nostalgic to me, and i would likely not be far off if i said i've listened to riff-rain and amp-reflection both approximately a hundred times over the course of middleschool. revisiting sfp again, riff-rain no matter how much dust is on it still sits comfortably in my top 5 albums whenever i make a list, and i'm sure a lot of is nostalgia but there is something in this album that i haven't found in any other. while prog-roid has a lot more 'bangers', riff-rain just grips me a lot deeper with tracks like [egoist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m75r54BwahQ) and the phenomenal opening track [flow](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K121OG3O70U). the blending of youthful and melancholic j-rock melodies with the electronic risers, effects, and piano segments just makes me keep coming back to this band every time.

## ping pong the animation

![](/_includes/img/pingpong.jpg)

i'm sorry i slept on this for so long. i knew i'd really like it but i did not expect it to be one of the three anime i've given a 10/10. it's getting late as i'm writing this and i don't think i could do it justice without a full designated blog post, but i will just say that the character writing is outstanding and the anime has a really interesting way of presenting almost every character as their 'face' which often is unpleasant and damning to a fault. it takes these small personal scenes to really open these characters up. the egotistical international player who just wants to move back to china and has no interest in playing 'inferior japanese players', and there's a moment where we learn about his relationship with his mother in china, and she sends him a package of sweets that accidentally has one of her new grey hairs bundled up in it- and suddenly you realise that maybe there's more at stake than we want to believe. everyone has these moments and they're full of charm and so deeply entangled in mundane yet deeply important human experiences.

the soundtrack too is by kensuke ushio (of silent voice and devilman fame) and absolutely no notes on that front as always, the animation is absolutely gorgeous, and all just goes to accompany the very special story already on display. to me, the anime ultimately is about effort and what it means to truly give something your all and what makes it worth all the fuss.

some extended thoughts: i wanted to cry at this film a lot. something about miso so earnestly seeing these people who were once her closest friends while they are changed, and honestly broken, and still seeing them as who they were deep down really hit a chord with me. i really liked the touch of her being a cleaner and the tragedy of her leaving every place spotless and without a trace, like she really feels like a phantom in their lives and never really wants to be anything grander- miso is presented like a total bum and i think is perhaps one of the kindest people i've seen in film who never tries to be nice.

i love films that make me walk away with a desire to change or live more in a certain way, and microhabitat came to me at a time where i am stressing so much about relationships, personal conflicts, housing, finances, my relationship with my friends and family, and my career. i should be a little more like miso, and maybe there is still time to enjoy cigarettes and whiskey, and to see the face of friends i haven't been able to meet in a while. i should try to make up with my old best friend i cut off years ago.
